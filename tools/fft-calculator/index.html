How to read the spectrum

The FFT separates a finite time record into frequency bins. Frequency resolution is Δf = fs/N. Windowing reduces leakage at the cost of a wider spectral peak; flat top is useful for amplitude estimates, while Hann is a robust general-purpose choice.
