Sound pressure and sound power are different quantities

Sound pressure level Lp describes the pressure fluctuation at a measurement position. It changes with distance, directivity, reflections and the acoustic environment. Sound power level LW describes the total acoustic power emitted by a source and is, in principle, independent of the measurement position.

Free-field estimation

The Lp–LW modes assume ideal geometric spreading from a compact source. The directivity factor Q accounts for radiation into a reduced solid angle. Additional attenuation A may represent known propagation losses, but room reflections and near-field effects are not included.

LW = 80 dB + 10 log10(4π · 1² / 2) = 87.98 dB

Interpret results carefully

Q = 1
Ideal source radiating into a full sphere.
Q = 2
Source on or above one ideal reflecting plane.
Standards
A standards-compliant sound power determination normally uses multiple positions or an enclosing measurement surface.
Limitations
Do not use this estimate as a substitute for ISO-standard measurements in regulatory or safety-critical work.
