Modulation envelope

A(t) = 1 + m · g(2πfmt + φ)

Amplitude modulation

x(t) = A · c(t) · [1 + m · g(2πfₘt + φ)]

The carrier c(t) may be periodic or noise. The modulator g ranges from −1 to +1, and m is the modulation depth. At 100% the envelope reaches zero. Above 100% it becomes negative for part of the cycle, producing overmodulation and phase inversion.
